Ancestry-Conditioned Rare Mutation Weighting & GRM Kinship Estimator

Weights shared variants inversely by background frequency. Rare, unique mutations (pv ≪ 1) receive exponentially higher positive scores, revealing true biological kinship rather than stochastic noise.

VAFi, v: Allele frequency of sample i
pv: Conditioned reference frequency
pv(1 − pv): Variance standardization
V: Polymorphic locus count
2. Ancestry Conditioning & Family Averaging Lineage Correction
p̂v(g) = λ pv(g) + (1 − λ) pvglobal

Prevents deep continental lineage markers from masquerading as close recent familial kinship. Multi-member families are averaged and counted exactly once when calculating pvglobal to prevent large cohorts from skewing background rates.

λ = 0.50: Haplogroup weight
pv(g): Haplogroup cohort mean
pvglobal: Global family-averaged rate

📐
Distance Metric Conversion: D(i, j) = max(K) − K(i, j)

Inverts the similarity matrix into a strictly non-negative, metric distance space with Dtree(i, i) = 0 for deterministic UPGMA phylogenetic tree clustering.

🛡️
Monomorphic Locus Elimination: 0 < pv < 1

Sites fixed at 0.0 or 1.0 across all lineages are automatically filtered, preventing division-by-zero singularities and ensuring finite, stable pairwise distances.
